dublin's secret sauna history, part 1.

1860: dr richard barter opened dublin's first purpose-built turkish baths on lincoln place, beside trinity college. james joyce even put it in ulysses. the photo is from around 1972, with the old baths' facade on the left.

1869: on st patrick's day barter opened the hammam hotel on sackville street (now o'connell street), a hotel with turkish baths out the back.

1906: the guinness family's iveagh trust opened the iveagh baths on bride road in the liberties, for people with no bathroom at home. it closed in 1985 and the building is a gym today.

1922: the civil war fighting on o'connell street destroyed the hammam on 5 july. it never reopened.

today: the forty foot in sandycove was men only until women 'invaded' it in july 1974. now dublin's coast is full of sea saunas.
